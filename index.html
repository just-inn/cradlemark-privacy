Cradlemark US launch privacy notice

UAB Ros albus in Lithuania operates the Cradlemark US launch list. You can reach us at info@rosalbus.com.

What you sign up for

When you ask for the launch email through our Facebook form, we receive your email address, your answer and the signup details Meta supplies, including the time and the form or ad you used. We use your consent to send you one email when the personalized pencil birth print is available in the US. Joining is free. We ask for an adult’s email address only.

Who handles the information

Meta hosts the form and gives authorized members of our launch team access to responses. Our email provider processes the address to deliver the notification. Meta describes how it handles information on its services in its privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep the launch list until we send the notification or cancel the launch, then delete our copy within 30 days. You can withdraw your consent before the email is sent.

Your choices

Email info@rosalbus.com to withdraw, ask for access or a copy, correct your entry, or request deletion. You may also contact your local data protection authority or the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate.
